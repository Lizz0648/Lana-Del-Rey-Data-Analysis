# Lana-Del-Rey-Data-Analysis
An interactive PowerBI dashboard analyzing sonic trends and musical shifts across Lana Del Rey's complete Spotify discography.
<img width="1323" height="718" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/4b05c53e-81cd-472e-98c4-13afb224181b" />
These charts show Lana Del Rey's artistic development through her albums over a decade. Obviously, her albums are eclectic based on these charts. For example, Lana's prior album to Lust For Life(release year: 2017) is called Honeymoon(release year: 2015) and as charts show, it has slower tempo, melancholic vibes and is moodier than Lust For Life. Tempo changes drastically from 2015 to 2017, Lust For Life is speedier and faster than her previous album.

Honeymoon stands out as the jazzy and cinematic piece of her art that's why its instrumentalness is at its peak compared to her other albums. 
Danceability metrics measure how suitable a track is for dancing, using a scale from  0.0(least danceable) to 1.0 (most danceable). This algorithmic score is determined by analyzing a song's tempo, rhythmic stability, beat strength, and overall regularity.
Liveness :  a metric that detects the presence of a live audience in a recording. It estimates the probability that a song was performed and recorded live versus being a traditional studio recording.
Emotionak Intensity : I calculated emotional intensity of songs by averaging its valence and energy levels.
